Journey

The longer version.

From community organizations to fast-scaling tech. The thread through all of it has been the same: getting the right people in place, and helping them work well together.

  1. 2024 — 2026

    HR Team Lead · HR Manager

    Anecdotes · Israel & US

    Started as the Israel site's HR manager, combining hands-on business partnering with ownership of global recruitment, then went on to lead a distributed HR team across Israel and the US. Partnered with managers on performance and complex people decisions, launched a monthly management forum and annual leadership offsites, and worked with R&D leadership to turn recurring team feedback into real decisions about structure and expectations.

  2. 2023 — 2024

    Global Talent Acquisition Manager · TA & Experience Manager

    Faye · Israel & Richmond, VA

    Joined as the first HR professional and built the People foundations while the company grew its headcount by 200% in a year. Led global talent acquisition through international expansion, set up the Richmond, Virginia site from the ground up, and put in place recruiting and compliance processes that scaled across locations.

  3. 2021 — 2023

    People Operations · Talent Acquisition Partner

    Seebo (now part of Augury)

    Recruited through a period of significant growth, adjusting hiring priorities as the company scaled toward its acquisition. Then broadened into People Operations: HRIS rollout, onboarding and offboarding, and compensation and benefits processes.

  4. 2018 — 2021

    Recruitment & Partnerships Manager

    Israel AIDS Task Force

    Worked where people, culture and community meet. Led recruitment and engagement for employees and volunteers, and partnered with donors and government stakeholders on funded programs, budgets and resource allocation.

  5. 2015 — 2018

    Program Development & Volunteer Management

    Israeli Volunteer Association

    Where it started: building people-centered programs, recruiting the volunteer teams to deliver them, and managing the partners and budgets behind them.

  6. Education

    Learning along the way

    Coaching · International relations · Business

    Adlerian Coaching Practitioner (PCIL), Adler Institute (2021–2023). M.A. with thesis in Social Sciences & International Relations, Heinrich-Heine-Universität Düsseldorf (2013–2015), including executive training at the Diplomatic Academy of Vienna. B.A. in Business Administration & Management, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev (2010–2013).
